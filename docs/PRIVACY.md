# Unearth Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 15 October 2026

Unearth (“we”) helps people share little-known public places on a map. This policy covers the Unearth iOS app, Android app, and website.

The short version: we collect only what Unearth needs to work. We don’t sell your data, we don’t show ads, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

Published at [unearth.vercel.app/privacy](https://unearth.vercel.app/privacy) and [unearth-1o4a.onrender.com/privacy](https://unearth-1o4a.onrender.com/privacy).

## What we collect

- **Account (iPhone):** Sign in with Apple identifier, and optional name/email Apple provides. Hide My Email gives us an Apple relay address.
- **Account (Android):** Google sign-in identifier, verified email, and the name on your Google account.
- **Account linking:** if you use Apple on iPhone and Google on Android with the same verified email, we link those sign-ins to one Unearth account. We do not auto-link Apple Hide My Email addresses.
- **Handle:** the username you choose, and the date you agreed to the Terms of Use.
- **Location (when you allow it):** your device location while you use Dig, so nearby gems and distances can be shown. When In Use / while-using access only — we do not track you in the background. We don’t store location history; the only locations we keep are gems you post.
- **Maps:** iPhone uses Apple Maps; Android uses Google Maps and Places, which may process map use and approximate location under Google’s policies.
- **Content you post:** gem names, categories, map pins, descriptions, liked/didn’t-like notes, tags, optional Google Maps links, stash saves, votes, reports, and blocks.
- **Technical:** authentication tokens on the device, and short-lived server logs (IP, device/app version, errors).

We don’t collect photos, contacts, payment information, or advertising identifiers.

## How we use it

- To run Dig, Unearth, Stash, Activity, and Profile.
- To show your finds and Prospectors level (and public handle/finds where the product exposes them).
- To detect duplicate places and keep Unearth safe (reports, Terms, spam).
- To revoke Apple or Google sign-in access if you delete your account, where possible.

## Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your handle and posted gems/finds are public to other Unearth users (Stash, reports, and blocks are private). We use providers solely to operate the service: Apple (Sign in with Apple, Maps), Google (sign-in, Maps, Places), Render (API hosting), Neon (database), and Vercel (website). Providers may store data outside India, including in Singapore.

## Retention & deletion

You can delete your account in Profile → Settings → Delete account, or request deletion at [unearth.vercel.app/delete-account](https://unearth.vercel.app/delete-account) (also at [unearth-1o4a.onrender.com/delete-account](https://unearth-1o4a.onrender.com/delete-account)). That removes your user record and associated content we store, and revokes Apple or Google token access where possible. Gems that other people also found remain, without your write-up.
